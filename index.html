Why Latin America. Why Colombia.

The case for looking south, and the rules behind it.

The benefits are real, and so are the risks. Here is what draws buyers from the US, Canada and Europe, with the rule or source behind each point.

Latin America

Close to home

Latin America shares or sits near North American time zones and is a short flight away, so owning or running something there is practical from home.

Latin America

Your currency goes further

Many local currencies trade cheaply against the dollar, euro and loonie, so the same income or savings can reach further. The rate can also move against you while you hold. How the exchange rate works.

Latin America

Markets others overlook

Political and structural risk is often priced in, sometimes more than it should be. Research tells the difference. The three arbitrages.

Why Colombia

  • Foreigners can own property with the same rights as Colombians. Source and detail
  • A foreign investment registered with Banco de la República carries the right to bring the money home. How registration works
  • Qualifying property can support an investor visa. Visa rules
  • Buildings run under a clear legal regime, propiedad horizontal. What it means for owners
  • Bogotá runs on UTC-5 all year, the same clock as US Eastern time in winter.

And what to watch

  • The peso can fall against your home currency while you hold. Currency risk
  • Taxes apply when you buy, while you own and when you sell. Taxes for foreigners
  • Money that arrives informally can be hard to take back out. Register it on the way in.
  • Policy changes with each government. How to read a country

Questions investors ask

Sourced answers before you commit.

All questions
Research

Can foreigners buy property in Colombia?

Yes, with the same ownership rights as Colombians, if the money comes in through the formal exchange market and is registered.

Research

Why do you have to register a foreign investment in Colombia?

Registration with Banco de la República is what protects your right to move the money and the sale proceeds back out.

Research

What taxes do foreigners pay on property in Colombia?

Closing costs when you buy, annual predial while you own, tax on rent, and 15% capital gains tax when you sell after two years.

Research

How does the exchange rate change your return on property abroad?

Your return is the local price change plus or minus the currency move. A strong local gain can disappear when converted home.

Straight answers

Questions people ask first.

What is Tierra Firme?

Tierra Firme is a consortium of three practitioners in commercial real estate, business and family capital, and intelligence research. It produces a free show, research and seminars for people placing capital across borders, starting with Latin America.

Who is behind Tierra Firme?

Murphy D. Cheatham II (commercial real estate), Shawn Christopher Leamon (business and family capital) and Todd Allyn (research). Each runs an independent practice and teaches his own lane.

What is the Investor Lab 2027?

A preliminary six-day, five-night program in Colombia for an exclusive group of investors: three days in Bogotá and two in Medellín, 7 to 12 March 2027. Short boardroom briefings, verified field visits and cultural evenings. It is a seminar, not a sales event. Details go to newsletter subscribers first.

Who is Tierra Firme for?

Individuals, families and family offices in the US, Canada and Europe who want to understand a market abroad before committing capital, including households priced out of ownership at home and retirees stretching a fixed income.

Is Tierra Firme free?

The show, the sourced answers and the Tierra Firme Newsletter are free. The Investor Lab is a paid seminar, and its details go to newsletter subscribers first.

What is the Tierra Firme Newsletter?

A free email from Tierra Firme: Investor Lab 2027 updates, short briefings on Colombia and Latin America across property, business and family capital, and country research, plus new answers and episodes. Unsubscribe in one click.

Does Tierra Firme sell property or arrange transactions?

No. Tierra Firme teaches how to read a market before you commit. It does not list property, arrange transactions or take fees on what anyone buys.

Is anything from Tierra Firme investment advice?

No. Everything Tierra Firme publishes is educational. It is not investment, tax or legal advice. Decisions stay with you and your own advisers.

Who leads the research?

Todd Allyn leads Tierra Firme research: the economic, currency and country conditions behind each episode and seminar. Murphy and Shawn each lead their own lanes.

Where is Tierra Firme based?

Tierra Firme is based in Dallas and Bogotá, and we work globally. The first seminar is in Bogotá, which holds the central bank, tax authority, notaries and ministries every foreign investor deals with.
