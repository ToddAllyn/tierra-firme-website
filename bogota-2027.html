The problem

Most buyers study the property. The country decides the outcome.

A good apartment in the wrong conditions is a poor decision. Three things move your real result more than the asset does, and most expat events skip all three.

Currency

Two currencies

You earn in one currency and own in another. The peso can add to what you keep or erase it before rent is ever collected.

Title and tax

Who owns what

How title and propiedad horizontal work, what you owe while you hold and what you owe when you sell.

Getting money out

The way out

Registering your investment correctly on the way in, so you can bring the money home on the way out.

Questions

Before you ask.

When and where is the Investor Lab?

Wednesday 10 to Friday 12 March 2027 in Bogotá, Colombia, at an upscale hotel in north Bogotá. The venue is confirmed to registered attendees.

Who is it for?

People who earn in dollars, euros or another currency and are weighing property or a small business in Colombia, and families thinking about how to hold what they build across borders.

How many seats are there?

Thirty. The room is kept small so every question gets answered.

Will anyone sell me property or a business?

No. There are no listings, no sponsors pitching from the stage and no referral fees. Site visits show places, not units for sale. It is education, not advice.
