Who it is for

Two audiences, one question.

  • Adults near or in retirement stretching a fixed income further abroad.
  • Earners with portable income building a first real asset abroad.
  • Families and family offices weighing Latin America for the first time.
How it runs

Filmed where it happens.

  • Filmed in a US studio and in market in Colombia, with the asset and the operator on camera.
  • Despacho: every episode cut again in Spanish for bilingual audiences across the Americas.
  • Short clips in English and Spanish for YouTube Shorts, Reels, TikTok and LinkedIn.
